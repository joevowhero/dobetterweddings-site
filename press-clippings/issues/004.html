The One Thing That Fixes 80% of Wedding Day Chaos

Two-Minute Drill by Joe Karsonovich

Quick one this week because the concept is simple but most grooms completely miss it.

The number one thing that takes stress off your bride between now and the wedding day is you understanding the timeline better than she does.

Your friends and family will have questions -- despite there being a whole wedding website -- and you need to be able to answer them without directing people to your bride.

When does the photographer arrive? When do the boys need to be dressed? When does the ceremony actually start? What happens right after?

You can see how quickly things can get out of hand.

But when you know the timeline cold, three things happen.

She stops having to answer your questions. Your groomsmen stop asking her questions because they go to you instead. And on the day itself you can actually be present instead of scrambling to figure out what comes next.

Most grooms treat the timeline like fine print, but the great ones treat it like a game plan.
